How to use this guide

Read once, then jump to your role whenever you need a reminder.

  1. Log in to Genesis Design with your username and password. You land on your Dashboard.
  2. Click your role card below (or use the top menu).
  3. Follow the numbered steps in order.
  4. Blue/grey boxes show the exact button names you will see in the app.
  5. Small Bangla text under a button is only a short label — the app buttons are in English.

Requester

অনুরোধকারী — Design Requester

Your job: Create a design request and choose a Site Engineer. Then track progress until the design is completed.

Dashboard Projects My Tasks Design Library Notifications
You are here Requester → Site Engineer
  1. 1

    Log in and open Dashboard

    Open Genesis Design, enter your username and password. Your home page is the Dashboard.

    WhereLogin page → Dashboard
    NextYou see your projects and request summary.
  2. 2

    Open your project

    Go to Projects in the left menu. Click the project you need a design for.

    WhereProjects → open project
    NextProject detail page opens.
  3. 3

    Start a new design request

    On the project page, click New Design Requestনতুন ডিজাইন অনুরোধ

    WhereProject detail page
    NextThe request form opens.
  4. 4

    Fill in the request details

    Choose drawing type, priority, target completion date, and write a clear description of what you need.

    WhereNew Design Request form
    TipDesign files are sent by email or digital format — not uploaded in this form.
  5. 5

    Choose a Site Engineer

    Select the Site Engineer who will visit the site. You can also set an engineer due date and instructions.

    WhereSite Engineer field on the form (required)
    NextThat person will get the first task.
  6. 6

    Submit the request

    Click Submit Requestঅনুরোধ জমা দিন

    What happensRequest goes to the Site Engineer for site verification.
  7. 7

    Track your request

    Open My Tasks to see your open requests. Click View → to open a request and check status and current holder.

    WhereMy Tasks → request detail
    NextStatus badges show where the request is in the pipeline.
  8. 8

    Watch notifications

    Open Notifications when status changes or someone needs information from you.

    WhereNotifications in the menu
    NoteEarly on, you may cancel a request only before Head of Design acknowledges it.

Site Engineer

সাইট ইঞ্জিনিয়ার

Important: Site Engineer is not a separate login role. It is an extra permission on a Requester, Verifier, or Compliance account. When a Requester assigns you, you work from My Tasks.

Dashboard My Tasks Notifications
You are here Site Engineer → Head of Design
  1. 1

    Open My Tasks

    Log in and go to My Tasks. Look for the Site Engineer section — designs assigned to you for site work.

    WhereMy Tasks (Site Engineer list)
    NextYou see design numbers waiting for you.
  2. 2

    Open the design request

    Click the design to open its detail page. Read the requester’s description and any engineer instructions.

    WhereDesign detail page — Actions panel on the right
  3. 3

    Acknowledge the assignment

    Click Acknowledge Site Assignmentসাইট অ্যাসাইনমেন্ট স্বীকার

    What happensStatus becomes engineer in progress — you can now submit site notes.
  4. 4

    Visit the site and gather notes

    Collect measurements, device locations, scenario notes, and anything the design team needs.

    TipWrite clear notes — Head of Design and Designer will rely on them.
  5. 5

    Submit your site review

    Click Submit Designডিজাইন জমা দিন then enter required Site Notes (measurements, device locations, scenario notes…).

    WhereActions panel → form with Site Notes field
    What happensRequest moves to Head of Design as a new request.

Head of Design

হেড অফ ডিজাইন — HOD

Your job: You run the workflow. Acknowledge requests, assign designers, review work, send to Verifier and Compliance, and mark designs completed.

Dashboard Projects My Tasks Taskboard Design Library Reports Notifications
You are here Head of Design → Designer / Verifier / Compliance
  1. 1

    Find new requests

    Open My Tasks or Dashboard. Open a request that is waiting after site engineer submission.

    WhereMy Tasks / Dashboard → design detail
  2. 2

    Acknowledge the request

    Click Acknowledgeস্বীকার করুন

    What happensRequest is acknowledged — you can assign a designer.
  3. 3

    Assign a designer

    Click Assign Designerডিজাইনার নিয়োগ Pick the designer, set due date, add instructions if needed, then save.

    WhereAssign Designer form
    What happensDesigner receives the assignment.
  4. 4

    Review submitted work

    When the designer submits, open the design detail page and review the submission.

    WhereMy Tasks → design detail (Actions panel)
  5. 5

    Send to verification or request correction

    If the work is good, click Send to Verificationযাচাইয়ে পাঠান and choose a verifier and due date. If changes are needed, click Request Correctionসংশোধন চান

    What happensVerification: goes to Verifier. Correction: designer must fix and resubmit.
  6. 6

    Handle verification corrections

    If the verifier requests correction, open the design and either Forward to Designerডিজাইনারের কাছে পাঠান or Re-assign Designerপুনরায় নিয়োগ You can also send to verification again after fixes.

  7. 7

    Send to compliance

    After the verifier accepts, click Send to Complianceকমপ্লায়েন্সে পাঠান Choose a compliance team member and due date.

    What happensCompliance officer reviews the design.
  8. 8

    Complete the design

    When compliance accepts, click Mark Completedসম্পন্ন চিহ্নিত করুন In some stages you may also use Fast-Track Completeদ্রুত সম্পন্ন when allowed.

    What happensDesign is completed. Requester can see the final status.

Designer

ডিজাইনার

Your job: Accept assignments, produce the design, submit work, and fix corrections when asked.

Dashboard Projects My Tasks Design Library Notifications
You are here Designer → Head of Design (review)
  1. 1

    Open your assignment

    Go to My Tasks and open the design assigned to you.

    WhereMy Tasks → design detail
  2. 2

    Accept the assignment

    Click Accept Assignmentঅ্যাসাইনমেন্ট গ্রহণ

    What happensStatus becomes in progress — you can work and submit.
  3. 3

    Do the design work

    Create the design using site notes and instructions. Design files are shared by email or digital format as your team requires.

    TipCheck due date and any HOD instructions on the detail page.
  4. 4

    Submit your work

    Click Submit Workকাজ জমা দিন

    What happensWork goes to Head of Design for review.
  5. 5

    Fix corrections and resubmit

    If status is Correction Required, update the design, then click Resubmitপুনরায় জমা দিন

    What happensHOD reviews again. You may get more correction rounds from verification or compliance (via HOD).
  6. 6

    Track until completed

    Use My Tasks and Notifications to follow status until Approved / Completed.

Verifier

যাচাইকারী — Verification Team

Your job: Acknowledge verification assignments, review the design, then accept or request correction.

Dashboard Projects My Tasks Design Library Notifications
You are here Verifier → Head of Design → Compliance
  1. 1

    Open designs waiting for you

    Go to My Tasks and open a design in the verification queue.

    WhereMy Tasks → design detail
  2. 2

    Acknowledge verification

    Click Acknowledge Verificationযাচাই স্বীকার করুন

    What happensYou can now accept or request correction.
  3. 3

    Review the design

    Check submissions, comments, and site notes on the detail page.

  4. 4

    Accept or request correction

    If the design is correct, click Acceptগ্রহণ করুন If changes are needed, click Request Correctionসংশোধন চান

    What happensAccept → Head of Design can send to Compliance. Correction → HOD routes back to the designer.
  5. 5

    After a correction

    Head of Design forwards work to the designer. When it returns for verification, you may see it again in My Tasks.

Compliance

কমপ্লায়েন্স — Compliance Team

Your job: Acknowledge compliance review, check the design against compliance rules, then accept or request correction.

Dashboard Projects My Tasks Design Library Notifications
You are here Compliance → Head of Design (Mark Completed)
  1. 1

    Open designs waiting for compliance

    Go to My Tasks and open a design in the compliance queue.

    WhereMy Tasks → design detail
  2. 2

    Acknowledge compliance review

    Click Acknowledge Compliance Reviewকমপ্লায়েন্স পর্যালোচনা স্বীকার

    What happensYou can now accept or request correction.
  3. 3

    Review for compliance

    Check the design against your compliance requirements on the detail page.

  4. 4

    Accept or request correction

    If compliant, click Acceptগ্রহণ করুন If not, click Request Correctionসংশোধন চান

    What happensAccept → design becomes Approved. Correction → HOD routes back to the designer.
  5. 5

    After you accept

    Head of Design clicks Mark Completed to finish the workflow. You do not need to complete it yourself.

Shared tips for everyone

সবার জন্য সহজ টিপস — Simple tips for all roles

1

Always start from My Tasks to see work waiting on you.

2

Open the design detail page for action buttons — they appear in the right-hand Actions panel.

3

Watch Notifications for new assignments and correction requests.

4

Status badges show where the request is in the pipeline (submitted, verification, compliance, approved, and so on).

5

Deadlines and overdue items appear in red on task lists — act on those first.
